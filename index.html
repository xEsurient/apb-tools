Config merge

Check a localisation config (e.g. a German .GER pack) against the game's English INT files: lists every line your config is missing, adds them at the right places and gives you the merged config as a zip, with a summary of what was added to which file. Your files never leave your browser.

Map viewer

Every district and map of the game in your browser: roam the 2D map (zoom, search, object details) or fly through the districts in 3D with a 2D minimap.
